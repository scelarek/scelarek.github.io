## 🏥 SSM Saint Louis University Hospital · Charge Nurse &amp; QI and Safety Council Chair · 2014 – 2019

- **Four years on the QI &amp; Safety Council, two as chair**, leading 6–8 nurses with pharmacy, physicians, infection control and Epic support, and presenting results to hospital leadership.
- **Central line infections (CLABSI) fell 41%**, from 2.32 to 1.37 per 1,000 line days (2017 to 2018 YTD), after house-wide adoption of central line care standards driven by infection data, protocol changes and chart audits.
- **Barcode medication scanning rose from 94.1% to 95.4%** (target 97%) with staff education, incentives and new equipment.
- Worked with Epic support on documentation audits and drop-down design for central line care, and trained nurses on new workflows at each Epic go-live.
- Charge nurse for staffing, acuity and escalations. Two-time DAISY Award winner.
